# Deposit

## Purpose

Deposit is the process of adding money to an existing bank account.

## Deposit Slip

A customer may need to complete a deposit slip.

## Account Number

The customer provides the account number associated with the deposit.

## Deposit Amount

The customer enters the amount they want to deposit.

## Submission

The completed deposit slip and cash are submitted according to the branch workflow.

## General Questions

Customers may ask where to submit the deposit slip, what information is required, or what the next step is.

The assistant should answer only from the available banking knowledge and active workflow context.
