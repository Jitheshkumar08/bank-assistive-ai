# Savings Account

## Purpose

A savings account is a deposit account intended for individuals to keep money safely and to save over time.

## Common Features

Money can be paid in and taken out, the balance is held by the bank, and interest may be paid on the balance.

## Typical Use

A savings account is commonly used for everyday personal banking: receiving income, keeping savings, and making deposits and withdrawals.

## Terms

Interest rates, minimum balance requirements, transaction limits, and charges depend on the bank, the account variant, and applicable regulations.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a savings account is, how it differs from a current account, or what it is used for.

The assistant should not state specific interest rates, minimum balances, limits, or charges unless they are available in the knowledge base.
