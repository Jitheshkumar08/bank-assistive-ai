# Withdrawal

## Purpose
Withdrawal is the process of taking money from an existing bank account.

## Withdrawal Slip
A customer may need to complete a withdrawal slip as part of the branch withdrawal process.

## Account Number
The customer needs to provide the account number associated with the withdrawal.

## Withdrawal Amount
The customer enters the amount they want to withdraw.

## Signature
The customer signs the withdrawal slip where required.

## Submission
The completed withdrawal slip and required cash-related documents are submitted according to the branch workflow.

## General Questions
Customers may ask where to submit the withdrawal slip, why a signature is required, what information needs to be entered, or what the next step is.

The assistant should answer only from the available banking knowledge and active workflow context.
