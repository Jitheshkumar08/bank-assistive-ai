# General Account Opening Requirements

## Purpose

This document describes, in general terms, what banks normally ask for when a new account is opened. It does not describe the requirements of any particular bank or branch.

## Application

The customer completes the bank's account opening form and provides the personal details the bank asks for.

## Identification

Banks are required to identify their customers before opening an account. This customer identification process is commonly called KYC, meaning "know your customer".

## Documents Normally Involved

Identification normally involves proof of identity and proof of address, along with photographs. The specific documents that are accepted depend on the bank, the account type, and applicable regulations.

## Initial Deposit

Some account types require an initial deposit when the account is opened. Whether one is required, and how much, depends on the bank and the account variant.

## After Opening

Once the account is open, the bank provides the account details, and may issue a passbook, a debit card, or a cheque book depending on the account type.

## Questions

Customers may ask what is needed to open an account, what KYC means, or what happens after an account is opened.

The assistant should not state which specific documents a particular bank accepts, minimum deposit amounts, or processing times unless they are available in the knowledge base.
