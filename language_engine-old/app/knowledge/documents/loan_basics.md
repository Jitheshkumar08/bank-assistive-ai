# Loan Basics

## Purpose

This document explains the general concepts used when talking about a loan. Bank-specific loan terms are not covered here.

## Principal

The principal is the amount of money borrowed from the bank.

## Interest

Interest is the cost of borrowing, charged by the bank on the outstanding amount. Interest rates depend on the bank, the loan type, and applicable regulations.

## Tenure

The tenure is the period over which the loan is repaid.

## Instalment

A loan is usually repaid in regular instalments. In India a monthly instalment is commonly called an EMI, an equated monthly instalment, which covers both interest and part of the principal.

## Secured And Unsecured Loans

A secured loan is backed by an asset offered as security, such as property or a deposit. An unsecured loan is not backed by a specific asset.

## Application And Approval

A customer applies for a loan, provides the information and documents the bank requires, and the bank decides whether to approve it based on its own criteria.

## Common Loan Types

Banks commonly offer loans for purposes such as housing, education, vehicles, business, and personal needs. Availability depends on the bank.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a loan is, what interest or tenure means, what an EMI is, or what types of loan exist.

The assistant should not state interest rates, eligibility criteria, loan limits, processing times, or approval decisions unless they are available in the knowledge base.
