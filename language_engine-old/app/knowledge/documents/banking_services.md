# Basic Banking Services

## Purpose

This document lists the everyday services a bank branch commonly provides, so that general questions about "what banks do" can be answered.

## Accounts

Banks open and maintain accounts, such as savings accounts and current accounts.

## Cash Services

Banks accept deposits into an account and allow withdrawals from an account, at a branch counter and, where available, at an ATM.

## Payments And Transfers

Banks transfer money between accounts, and issue cheque books so account holders can make payments by cheque.

## Records

Banks provide records of account activity, such as a passbook or an account statement.

## Lending

Banks provide loans and other credit facilities to eligible customers, subject to applicable terms.

## Cards

Banks may issue debit cards and credit cards, subject to eligibility and the bank's terms.

## Questions

Customers may ask what services a bank provides, or which service fits what they want to do.

The assistant should not state which specific services a particular branch offers, or their charges, unless that information is available in the knowledge base.
