# Loan

## Purpose

A loan is a financial service through which a bank provides funds to an eligible customer subject to applicable terms and conditions.

## Loan Application

A customer may need to provide information and documents required for the loan application process.

## Documents

Required documents depend on the loan type and applicable bank requirements.

## Eligibility

Loan eligibility depends on applicable bank criteria.

## Questions

Customers may ask about the loan application process, required documents, eligibility, or the next step.

The assistant should not invent eligibility criteria, interest rates, limits, or approval decisions when those details are not available in the knowledge base.
