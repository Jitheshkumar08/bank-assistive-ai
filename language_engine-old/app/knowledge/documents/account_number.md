# Account Number

## Purpose

An account number is the identifier the bank uses for a specific account. It tells the bank which account money should be paid into or taken out of.

## Where It Is Used

An account number is written on deposit slips and withdrawal slips, and is required when money is transferred to an account. It is also shown on a passbook, an account statement, and in a bank's online or mobile service.

## Accuracy

An account number must be entered exactly. An incorrect account number can send money to the wrong account or cause the request to be rejected.

## Related Identifiers

An account number identifies the account. A branch or bank identifier, such as a branch code, identifies where the account is held. A customer identifier is separate again and identifies the customer rather than one account.

## Questions

Customers may ask what an account number is, where to find it, or why it is needed on a form.
