# Withdrawal Slip

## Purpose

A withdrawal slip is the form used at a bank branch to take money out of an account in person.

## Information Normally Recorded

A withdrawal slip normally records the account number, the amount being withdrawn, the date, and the account holder's signature. The signature is what authorises the bank to release the money.

## How It Is Used

The customer completes the slip, signs it where required, and submits it at the branch counter, following the branch's process.

## Difference From A Deposit Slip

A withdrawal slip is used to take money out. A deposit slip is used to pay money in.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a withdrawal slip is, why a signature is required, what information it needs, or where it is submitted.
