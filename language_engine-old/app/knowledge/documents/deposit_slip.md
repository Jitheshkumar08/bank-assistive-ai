# Deposit Slip

## Purpose

A deposit slip, also called a paying-in slip, is the form used at a bank branch to pay money into an account.

## Information Normally Recorded

A deposit slip normally records the account number the money is being paid into, the amount being deposited, the date, and the name of the person making the deposit. Some banks also ask for a contact number or a signature.

## How It Is Used

The customer completes the slip, and submits it with the cash at the branch counter, following the branch's process.

## Difference From A Withdrawal Slip

A deposit slip is used to pay money in. A withdrawal slip is used to take money out.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a deposit slip is, what information it needs, or where it is submitted.
