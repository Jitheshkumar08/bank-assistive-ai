# Cheque Book

## Purpose

A cheque book is a booklet of printed cheques issued by a bank to an account holder. It is also written as "check book" in American usage, and is sometimes pronounced in ways that sound like "check book".

## What A Cheque Is

A cheque is a written instruction to the bank to pay a stated amount from the account holder's account to the person or organisation named on the cheque.

## Common Use

Cheques are used to make payments without handing over cash. The person who writes the cheque fills in the payee name, the amount, and the date, and signs it.

## Requesting A Cheque Book

An account holder may request a cheque book from their bank. Whether a cheque book is available, how long it takes, and any applicable charges depend on the bank and the type of account.

## Safekeeping

A cheque book should be kept safely, because a cheque can authorise a payment from the account.
