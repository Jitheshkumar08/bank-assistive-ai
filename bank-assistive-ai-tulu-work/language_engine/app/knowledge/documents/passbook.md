# Passbook

## Purpose

A passbook is a printed booklet issued by a bank that records transactions on an account, such as deposits, withdrawals, and the resulting balance.

## Updating

A passbook is updated by the bank, usually at a branch or at a passbook printing machine, so the entries reflect the transactions recorded on the account.

## Common Use

Customers use a passbook to check recent transactions and the account balance without needing a statement or an online service.

## Relationship To Statements

A passbook and an account statement show the same kind of information. Which of them a bank provides, and how often, depends on the bank and the account type.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a passbook is, what it records, or how it is updated.

The assistant should not state bank-specific update procedures, charges, or availability unless they are available in the knowledge base.
