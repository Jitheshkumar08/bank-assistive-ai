# Current Account

## Purpose

A current account is a deposit account intended mainly for businesses, firms, and customers who make frequent transactions.

## Common Features

A current account normally supports a high number of transactions. Interest is typically not the purpose of this account type, and an overdraft facility may be available subject to the bank's terms.

## Difference From A Savings Account

A savings account is intended for personal saving, while a current account is intended for frequent business transactions. The exact features of each depend on the bank.

## Terms

Charges, transaction limits, minimum balance requirements, and overdraft availability depend on the bank and applicable regulations.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a current account is, or how it differs from a savings account.
