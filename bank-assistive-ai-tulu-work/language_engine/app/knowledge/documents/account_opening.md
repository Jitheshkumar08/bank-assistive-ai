# Account Opening

## Purpose

Account opening is the process of creating a new bank account.

## Customer Information

The customer may need to provide personal and account-related information required by the bank.

## Documents

Required documents depend on the account type and applicable bank requirements.

## Application

The customer completes the applicable account-opening process and submits the required information.

## Questions

Customers may ask about the account-opening process, required information, documents, or the next step.

The assistant should not invent bank-specific requirements when they are not available in the knowledge base.
