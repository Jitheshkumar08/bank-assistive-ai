# Bank Transfer

## Purpose

A bank transfer moves money from one bank account to another without handing over cash.

## Information Normally Required

A transfer normally requires the beneficiary's account number, the beneficiary's name, and an identifier for the beneficiary's bank or branch.

## How Transfers Are Made

A transfer can be requested at a branch using the bank's form, or made electronically through the bank's online service, mobile application, or an ATM where that is supported.

## Electronic Transfer Systems

In India, electronic transfers are commonly made through systems such as NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, and UPI. Which of these a customer can use, along with any limits, charges, and processing times, depends on the bank and the service.

## Questions

Customers may ask what a bank transfer is, what details are needed, or how a transfer can be made.

The assistant should not state transfer limits, charges, or processing times unless they are available in the knowledge base.
