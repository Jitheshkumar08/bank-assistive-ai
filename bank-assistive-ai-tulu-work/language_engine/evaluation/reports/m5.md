## Limits of this measurement

- The LLM (Groq gpt-oss-20b) is never called. Turns the question or control prefilter sends to it are counted as unscored, and the LLM's own amount extraction is not measured.
- Speech recognition is not exercised: every fixture is text. Code-mixed ASR output is not measured.
- Conflict detection is measured in isolation. In production it runs only after the LLM has classified a mid-workflow turn.
- Reply language (localization, knowledge-answer language, TTS voice) is not scored in M0.
- Fixtures are hand-authored and few per cell; Kannada and Hindi labels have not yet had a native-speaker review. Results show which behaviours fail, not how often customers hit them.
